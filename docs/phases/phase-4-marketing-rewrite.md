# Phase 4: Marketing Rewrite

## Goal
Realign all public-facing, logged-out pages to reflect the new minimal aesthetic, highlighting extreme simplicity, zero mental clutter, and the privacy of an offline-first app.

## Detail Steps

1. **`landing.html.erb` Refresh**
   - Overhaul the main hero section to showcase the new flat, horizontal weekly planner. Remove outdated screenshots of "notebooks".
   - **Copy Changes**: 
     - Shift from "Beautiful hand-crafted notebook pages" to "The simplest calendar-based todo app. 100% Yours."
     - Emphasize attributes like "Reduced Mental Clutter," "Offline-first," and "Zero-Cloud."

2. **Secondary Pages (`pricing.html.erb`, `why.html.erb`)**
   - Maintain the narrative of a single purchase, no subscription lock-in.
   - Add copy detailing how extreme minimalism prevents user fatigue.
   - Ensure visual consistency (flush layouts, sans-serif fonts) trickles down fully across all marketing UI components to match the app interior.

3. **Final Visual Checks**
   - Verify meta-tags, hero images, and OpenGraph images are updated to represent the new "Week-View" screenshot.
   - Run system tests on the marketing flow (sign in, sign up, reading pages) to verify no dead links or broken layouts exist.
